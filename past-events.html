SK Cannabis Connect 2026

A trade show by day, a networking night after. One hundred and fifty people in one room: 20 licensed producers exhibiting, and roughly 75 of Saskatchewan’s retailers on the floor. The day ran as an LP Showcase, and the evening closed with the Buyer Appreciation Party.

LP Showcase floor — Saskatoon, June 2026
By day

LP Showcase

Three hours on the floor with the top producers in the province. Connect, pick up swag, and learn the products you already carry — straight from the people who make them.

By night

Buyer Appreciation Party

Food, networking, connection. A light, fun close to the day — with comedian and Canada’s Got Talent golden buzzer winner Sterling Scott, and caricatures drawn on the spot by Simon Glassman.
